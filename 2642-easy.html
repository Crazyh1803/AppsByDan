Medical claim paperwork, made easier

2642 Easy

Prepare your DD Form 2642 claim packet, bring the supporting documents together, and keep track of what happens next.

Open web app View on GitHub

Web app available now · Google Play — coming soon

One place for your claim packet.

Built for military families preparing medical reimbursement claims. Save family profiles to fill repeated details, sign on screen, and bring your form, bills and receipts together.

Use it in your browser on a computer, phone or tablet. The Android app is coming to Google Play.

From paperwork to a prepared packet

Fill and sign

Choose a family profile, enter the claim details, and draw your signature with a finger, mouse or pen. The app fills DD Form 2642.

Add your documents

Capture pages with your camera or upload photos and PDFs. Organize each attachment as a bill or receipt, then export one combined PDF or separate form, bill and receipt files.

Track what’s next

Search and filter claims by patient or status. Mark claims as filed online or reimbursed, and export a backup to keep your records or move between the web and Android versions.

Your records stay on your device.

The web app processes your claim details and documents in your browser. Saved records are encrypted and protected by the passcode you choose.

Export a backup regularly. Clearing browser data removes your local records, and a forgotten passcode cannot be recovered.

2642 Easy is an independent document preparation tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by TRICARE, DHA or DoD. You submit the finished packet to your claims processor or portal; signature acceptance is determined by your claims processor.
