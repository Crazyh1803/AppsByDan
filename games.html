Gaga Pit Showdown

One ball. No teams. Last one standing.

The schoolyard gaga pit, in pixel art. Duck, swat, and survive the pit until there’s nobody left to knock out.

Slap the ball, hold for a power shot, and dash through incoming hits as the CPU tries to knock you out of an octagonal pit. Runs right in the browser, on a phone or a desktop — no install, no account.

Play in your browser

Want it on your phone as an app? It’s on Google Play in closed testing, and both steps are needed:

1. Join the group 2. Install the test

Group page not loading, or signed in to more than one Google account? Send a blank email to join from the account you use on the Play Store.
