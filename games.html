Games

Two games to play right now in your browser. Same deal as the apps: free, no ads, nothing locked off.

Out now

Hedge and Harvest

Carve a little art. Grow a little adventure.

A cozy cornfield playground. Drive your tractor to carve pictures and paths, then turn your creation into a maze and explore it as a farm animal.

Choose a season, make your own maze or generate one, and export a picture or an editable creation to share. Play with arrow keys, WASD, dragging, or the on-screen controls — no install or account.

Explore Hedge and Harvest View source

Free to play. Your edits save in this browser; export a creation file to keep a backup or share with a friend.

Gaga Pit Showdown

One ball. No teams. Last one standing.

The schoolyard gaga pit, in pixel art. Duck, swat, and survive the pit until there’s nobody left to knock out.

Slap the ball, hold for a power shot, and dash through incoming hits as the CPU tries to knock you out of an octagonal pit. Runs right in the browser, on a phone or a desktop — no install, no account.

Play in your browser

Want it on your phone as an app? It’s on Google Play in closed testing, and both steps are needed:

1. Join the group 2. Download the app

If the store page says the item wasn’t found, Google hasn’t recognised you as a tester yet — give it a few minutes, or use the tester opt-in page. If the group page won’t load, or you’re signed in to more than one Google account, send a blank email to join from the account you use on the Play Store.
