True Shuffle

Real shuffle. No repeats. No bias.

Spotify’s shuffle keeps handing you the same forty songs. True Shuffle builds a playlist that spreads evenly across every artist in your library — including the ones you forgot you saved — and it remembers recent builds, so each one comes back different.

Manual Bridge

Download. UnLock. Ask AI.

TRICARE publishes its manuals as a maze of HTML frames you can’t comfortably read, search, or print. Manual Bridge pulls them into clean documents you can actually work with, refreshed weekly from the official DHA site.
