Hi, I’m Dan.

One person, a handful of small apps, and a habit of building the thing I wished already existed.

Almost everything here started the same way: something annoyed me, no app fixed it the way I wanted, so I built the one I wanted. Then it turned out other people wanted the same thing. That’s the whole model — I make things I want, and share them with anyone who likes the idea too.

The apps are free. No ads, no account you don’t need, no feature held back behind a paywall. If one of them is useful to you and you want to throw a few dollars my way, that’s what the tip link at the bottom of every page is for — and it’s genuinely what shapes what gets built next. Tips buy the time for extra features, so if you want one of these apps to do something it doesn’t do yet, send the idea along with the tip and it goes to the top of my list for consideration. I can’t promise every idea gets built — some won’t fit the app, and some I won’t be able to pull off — but a tipped idea is the first one I look at.

A note on how these get made: I use AI tools to build everything here. It lets me bring my imagination to life in ways that wouldn’t be possible without it, and helps me solve problems I face in everyday life in ways I would never have imagined possible. I decide what gets built, and I test it myself before it goes out.

Got an idea, a bug, or a question? Email me at odysseypages@gmail.com — ideas are welcome with or without a tip, and if one clicks I might well build it. I post what I’m working on at @appsbydan on Instagram and @appsbydan on TikTok, and you can poke around the source on GitHub.

Collaborate / Open call

Want to replace the synthetic stuff with something real?

The mechanics work, the gameplay loops are in place, and the builds are playable. You can try the games right now.

I currently use AI for rapid prototyping across code, visuals, and audio. I want to replace the generated art and sound with human artistry — and give these games a creative identity of their own.

If you’re an artist, animator, musician, or sound creator, I’d love to work with you. Any style or medium is welcome. Help shape the game’s creative identity and what it becomes.

  • Your assets stay yours. You retain 100% ownership of what you create, including the right to show it in your portfolio.
  • Equal credit. Every creator gets equal credit in-game and on the game’s web pages.
  • Equal revenue share. Any future income from the game, including tips specifically for that game, will be shared equally among all collaborators, including me: 50% each for two people, one third each for three. Before work starts, we’ll agree in writing on what income is included, how any fees or expenses are handled, and when payments are made, with transparent records everyone can see.
