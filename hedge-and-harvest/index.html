How to play Hedge and Harvest

  1. Carve. The field starts fully planted. Drive the tractor with the arrow keys / WASD, the on-screen pad, or by dragging from the tractor to cut paths and make corn art.
  2. Maze (optional). Turn on Maze to reveal Place start and Place exit. Choose a button, then tap or click any spot in the field. Nothing is generated for you unless you press Grow new maze, and your art is kept when you switch Maze on or off.
  3. Run. Once both gates are placed, switch to Run (or press M) and guide your animal along the cleared path to the coral flag. Corn blocks the way. The Route light turns green when a way through exists.
  4. U or Ctrl+Z undoes (including gate placement). In a maze, H shows a hint and R resets the runner.
  5. The whole field is shown by default; use View for an optional close-up. Each visit starts with a fresh planted field; your last carving is kept on this device and can be reopened with Continue. Export JSON or a PNG to share what you build.
